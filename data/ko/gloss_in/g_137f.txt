City (pop., 2020: 270,871) in Ohio, United States, and county seat of Lucas County. Located at the western end of [[Lake Erie]] on the Maumee River, its port is the fifth-busiest on the [[Great Lakes]]. Founded in 1833, it grew after the 1845 completion of the Miami and Erie Canal and lay on the railway line between [[New York City]] and [[Chicago]]. It was nicknamed the Glass City after glass manufacturers arrived in the 1880s. After World War II it turned largely to automotive production, and its population has declined from its peak amid [[Rust Belt]] deindustrialization.

ENTRY:
City and municipality in central [[Spain]] and capital of Toledo province (pop., 2024: 86,526). Built on a Carpetanian settlement, it became a Roman city and later the capital of the [[Visigothic Kingdom]]. Under Islamic rule it was a center of exchange between the Islamic world and Latin Christendom, and Christian forces took it in 1085. After losing political power to [[Madrid]], its economy stayed afloat on the [[Silk|silk industry]] until it declined from the 1630s. The city is known for its Gothic cathedral and bladed weapons. [[UNESCO]] declared it a [[World Heritage Site]] in 1986.

=== KEY: Toluene_e65b4f
HEADWORD: Toluene
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Gasoline | TNT
ENTRY:
Colorless, water-insoluble aromatic hydrocarbon, C₆H₅CH₃, also called toluol or methylbenzene. It has a sweet smell and contributes to the aroma of [[Gasoline|gasoline]] and paint fumes. It is mainly used as an industrial feedstock and solvent, including in some paint thinners and glues. Nitration of toluene yields explosives such as trinitrotoluene ([[TNT]]). It was first isolated in 1837 and named *toluène* by Auguste Cahours in 1850. Recreational inhalation can cause severe neurological harm.

American football quarterback. Brady played 23 seasons in the [[National Football League|National Football League (NFL)]], 20 of them with the [[New England Patriots]] and his last three with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Selected 199th overall in the sixth round of the 2000 NFL draft, he became the Patriots' starter in his second season and led them to their first [[Super Bowl|Super Bowl XXXVI]] title. American writer of techno-thrillers that feature technical detail in [[Espionage|espionage]] and [[Military science|military science]] settings. Originally an [[Insurance|insurance agent]], he published his first novel, *The Hunt for Red October*, in 1984. Eighteen novels followed between 1986 and 2013. His Ryanverse series, featuring analyst Jack Ryan of the [[Central Intelligence Agency]], is among the best-selling fiction series, with more than 100 million copies in print. Clancy also wrote nonfiction on military subjects, and his creations inspired video games such as *Rainbow Six* and *Splinter Cell*.
