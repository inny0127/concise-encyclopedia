=== KEY: Tom_Stoppard_66360e
HEADWORD: Stoppard, Tom
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Czechoslovakia | George Bernard Shaw | William Shakespeare
ENTRY:
British playwright and screenwriter, born in [[Czechoslovakia]]. He left as a Jewish child refugee fleeing Nazi occupation and settled in England in 1946. He became a journalist and drama critic before turning to playwriting in 1960. Critics compared him with [[William Shakespeare]] and [[George Bernard Shaw]]. His plays include *Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead* (1966), *Arcadia* (1993) and *Leopoldstadt* (2020). He won an Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay for *Shakespeare in Love*, and he was knighted in 1997.

American animated comedy franchise created by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera in 1940. Its 161 theatrical shorts were produced by [[Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer]] and center on a cat named Tom and a [[House mouse|house mouse]] named Jerry. Hanna and Barbera's MGM shorts won seven [[Academy Awards]] for Best Animated Short Film. After MGM closed its cartoon studio in 1957, the series was revived with shorts made in [[Czechoslovakia]] from 1961 to 1962, and the series now totals 166 shorts. The franchise also includes television series, a 1992 feature film, *Tom and Jerry: The Movie*, and a 2021 live-action/animated hybrid film.

=== KEY: Tom_s_de_Torquemada_ec30aa
HEADWORD: Torquemada, Tomás de
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Ferdinand II of Aragon | Isabella I of Castile | Kingdom of Castile | Seville | Spanish Inquisition
ENTRY:
Spanish Dominican friar and the first Grand Inquisitor of the [[Spanish Inquisition]]. He led the inquisition created in 1478 to uphold Catholic orthodoxy in the union of [[Kingdom of Castile|Castile]] and Aragon. He was confessor to Queen [[Isabella I of Castile|Isabella I]] and also became confessor to King [[Ferdinand II of Aragon|Ferdinand]]. Torquemada regarded converts from Judaism as a threat to religious and social life, and he was a chief supporter of the Alhambra Decree, which expelled the Jews from Spain in 1492. The Inquisition used torture to extract confessions and burned those declared guilty at the stake, and Torquemada approved of these practices. His name has become synonymous with cruelty, religious intolerance and fanaticism. At a general assembly in [[Seville]], he promulgated 28 articles of faith to guide the inquisitors' investigations.

=== KEY: Tomato_77bd79
HEADWORD: Tomato
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Aztecs | Berry (botany) | Columbian exchange | Ketchup | Pasta | Pizza | Solanaceae
ENTRY:
Edible [[Berry (botany)|berry]] of the plant *Solanum lycopersicum*, a member of the [[Solanaceae|nightshade family]] that also includes tobacco, potato and chili peppers. The tomato originated in western South America and may have been domesticated there, in Mexico or in Central America. By 500 BCE it was cultivated in southern Mexico, and the [[Aztecs]] raised several varieties. The Spanish introduced tomatoes to Eurasia in the [[Columbian exchange]] in the 16th century, where they were at first grown as ornamentals. Today the tomato is a staple of cuisines worldwide, used in [[Pasta|pasta]] and [[Pizza|pizza]] sauces, soups, salsas and [[Ketchup|ketchup]]. Modern cultivars, bred to ripen uniformly red, number in the thousands, though breeding has reduced their sweetness. Global production was approximately 182 million tons in 2022.

Action-adventure video game franchise, with films, [[Comics|comics]] and novels, centered on the British archaeologist Lara Croft. The first game, developed by British studio Core Design, appeared in 1996 for [[Sega Saturn]], [[PlayStation (console)|PlayStation]] and [[Personal computer|personal computers]]. Live-action films starring [[Angelina Jolie]] appeared in 2001 and 2003, and a 2018 film starred [[Alicia Vikander]].
