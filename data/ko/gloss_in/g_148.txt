ENTRY:
Chinese empress (r. 690–705), the only undisputed female sovereign in Chinese history. Wu was a concubine of Emperor Taizong, then empress consort of Emperor Gaozong from 655. After Gaozong suffered a stroke in 660, she effectively ruled until his death in 683, then governed for her sons. In 690 she proclaimed the [[Zhou dynasty]] in place of the [[Tang dynasty]] and crowned herself emperor. Over a 45-year de facto rule she used informants and strict legal measures, purged members of the Tang royal house, reformed the civil service to promote merit, and patronized [[Buddhism]], literature and the arts. Her rule formed part of the High Tang period, though her later governance became increasingly autocratic. She was removed in the Shenlong Coup, which restored the Tang, and died a few months later.

=== KEY: X86_64_402a5c

ENTRY:
64-bit extension of the [[X86|x86]] instruction set, also known as AMD64. Announced in 1999 and first available in the AMD Opteron family in 2003, it adds a 64-bit mode with larger memory addressing and increases the general-purpose registers from 8 to 16. A compatibility mode lets 16-bit and 32-bit applications run unmodified alongside 64-bit programs. The original specification, created by AMD and released in 2000, was implemented by [[Intel]] and VIA Technologies. It was quickly adopted for desktop, laptop and server computers, and it effectively replaced the discontinued Intel Itanium architecture, with which it is not compatible.

=== KEY: X_ray_dbc6d5

ENTRY:
Form of high-energy electromagnetic radiation with a wavelength shorter than ultraviolet and longer than gamma rays. X-rays have wavelengths from 10 nanometers to 10 picometers and photon energies from 100 eV to 100 keV. Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen discovered them in 1895 and named them X-radiation for an unknown type of radiation. They penetrate many solid substances, so [[Radiography|X-ray radiography]] is widely used in medical diagnostics, such as checking for broken bones, and in materials science. X-rays are ionizing radiation, and exposure can cause DNA damage, cancer and, at higher intensities, burns and radiation sickness. Their generation and use are strictly controlled by public health authorities.

Chemical element (symbol Xe) with atomic number 54. Xenon is a dense, colorless, odorless [[Noble gas|noble gas]] found in Earth's atmosphere in trace amounts. Long considered inert, it formed the first known noble gas compound, xenon hexafluoroplatinate, when Neil Bartlett mixed xenon with platinum hexafluoride on March 23, 1962. === KEY: Xia_dynasty_04ff98
HEADWORD: Xia dynasty
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Shang dynasty | Sima Qian | Yellow River | Yu the Great
ENTRY:
Traditional first dynasty in Chinese historiography. A majority of Chinese archaeologists regard it as historical and identify it with the Erlitou culture (c. 1900–1500 BC), while many Western historians and Sinologists consider it legendary or unsubstantiated. Tradition holds that [[Yu the Great]] founded it and that the [[Shang dynasty]] succeeded it. No contemporaneous records mention the Xia; the earliest references occur in the *Book of Documents*, whose oldest chapters report speeches from the early Western Zhou period. Estimated dates vary: the traditional chronology gives 2205–1766 BC, a chronology based on the Bamboo Annals gives 1989–1558 BC, and the Xia–Shang–Zhou Chronology Project, commissioned by the Chinese government in 1996, proposed 2070–1600 BC. The *Shiji* of [[Sima Qian]] ties the dynasty's origins to flood control on the [[Yellow River]], in which Yu's father Gun failed and Yu succeeded.

Percussion instrument of wooden bars struck with mallets, each bar an idiophone tuned to a pitch of a [[Scale (music)|musical scale]]. The name comes from Greek words meaning "wood" and "sound." The term may broadly include instruments such as the marimba and balafon, but in the [[Orchestra|orchestra]] it refers specifically to a chromatic instrument with a higher pitch range and drier timbre than the marimba. === KEY: Year_537c66
HEADWORD: Year
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Axial tilt | Earth | Eastern Orthodox Church | Gregorian calendar | Islamic calendar | Julian calendar | Leap year | Sun
ENTRY:
Unit of time based on how long it takes the [[Earth]] to orbit the [[Sun]]. In scientific use, the tropical year, about 365 days, 5 hours, and 48 minutes (365.2422 mean solar days), and the sidereal year, about 20 minutes longer, are more exact. Because of the planet's [[Axial tilt|axial tilt]], a year brings the seasons; temperate regions generally recognize four, while the seasonal tropics track wet and dry seasons.

A calendar year approximates the orbital period in whole days. The [[Gregorian calendar]] uses common years of 365 days and [[Leap year|leap years]] of 366; 97 of every 400 years are leap years, giving a mean year of 365.2425 days. In the [[Julian calendar]] the mean year is 365.25 days, with a leap day added every fourth year. The Revised Julian calendar, proposed in 1923 and used in some [[Eastern Orthodox Church|Eastern Orthodox churches]], has 218 leap years every 900 years. The [[Islamic calendar]] has twelve lunar months and ignores the solar year.

The word is also applied to other periods of similar duration: the lunar year (about 354 days), the seasonal year, the fiscal year, and the academic year. It can refer to the orbital period of any astronomical object, such as the Martian year of roughly 1.88 Earth years. The unit is abbreviated y or yr; scientific literature sometimes uses the symbol a, from Latin *annus*. English *year* descends from West Saxon *ġēar* and Proto-Germanic *jǣran*.

=== KEY: Yoruba_people_3a4143

ENTRY:
West African ethnic group inhabiting Yorubaland in parts of [[Nigeria]], [[Benin]] and [[Togo]]. The Yoruba number more than 50 million in Africa and over a million elsewhere, most of them in Nigeria, where they make up 20.7 percent of the population according to Ethnologue estimates. Most speak the [[Yoruba language]]. The Yoruba emerged largely in situ from earlier Volta-Niger populations by the 1st millennium BCE, and by the 5th century BCE a powerful city-state existed at Ile-Ife. The Ife Empire, the first empire in Yoruba history, flourished between 1200 and 1420 CE across much of southwestern Nigeria, eastern Benin and Togo. Later, the [[Oyo Empire]] dominated regional trade and military affairs from the 17th to early 19th centuries. Yoruba communities are also represented in the African diaspora in [[Brazil]], [[Cuba]] and the Caribbean.

=== KEY: Yoruba_religion_ce8b5b
HEADWORD: Yoruba religion
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Benin | Brazil | Cuba | Divination | Nigeria | Reincarnation | Togo | Yoruba people
ENTRY:
Traditional religious and spiritual system of the [[Yoruba people]], also called Orisha or Isese. Its homeland is Yorubaland in southwestern [[Nigeria]], southern [[Benin]] and central [[Togo]]. It is considered the largest indigenous African religion, with several million adherents worldwide. It is a form of diffused monotheism, with a supreme but distant creator force served by intermediary orishas and by ancestors. The Yoruba believe in the possibility of [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] within the family. Central practice is Ifá [[Divination|divination]], which consults verses through binary patterns inscribed on boards or palm nuts. The religion shares parallels with Vodun and is the basis for several religions in the New World, notably Santería in [[Cuba]] and Candomblé in [[Brazil]].
