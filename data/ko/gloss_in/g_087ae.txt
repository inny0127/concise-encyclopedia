=== KEY: Meiosis_ed87e3
HEADWORD: Meiosis
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Chromosome | Down syndrome | Fertilisation
ENTRY:
Cell division that produces haploid gametes, such as sperm and egg cells, in sexually reproducing organisms. Meiosis consists of two rounds of division that produce four cells, each with one copy of each [[Chromosome|chromosome]]. Before division, DNA is replicated and homologous chromosomes pair and exchange genetic material by recombination, which creates new combinations of genes. Because two haploid gametes fuse at [[Fertilisation|fertilization]], alternating meiosis and fertilization sustain sexual reproduction in animals, plants and fungi. Errors that produce an abnormal number of chromosomes, known as aneuploidy, are the leading known cause of miscarriage and the most frequent genetic cause of developmental disabilities, including [[Down syndrome]].

=== KEY: Mekong_734409
HEADWORD: Mekong
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Cambodia | Laos | Myanmar | Southeast Asia | Thailand | Tibet | Tibetan Plateau | Vietnam
ENTRY:
Transboundary river of East and [[Southeast Asia|Southeast Asia]], the world's twelfth-longest river. It rises on the [[Tibetan Plateau]], where it is called the Lancang River in China, and flows through [[Myanmar]], [[Laos]], [[Thailand]], [[Cambodia]] and southern [[Vietnam]]. Its estimated length is 4,909 km and its drainage area is 795,000 sq km, and it discharges 475 km³ of water annually. The name Mae Nam Khong combines *mae nam* ("mother of water[s]"), used in Thai and Lao for large rivers, with *khong*, an archaic word for river. Rapids, waterfalls and extreme seasonal variations make navigation difficult, although the river remains a major trade route between [[Tibet]] and Southeast Asia. Dams built along the river since the 2000s have caused serious ecosystem problems, including exacerbated drought.

=== KEY: Mel_Blanc_789ed4

PERSON: yes

ENTRY:
American voice actor and radio personality, known as "The Man of a Thousand Voices." Blanc provided voices for many [[Looney Tunes]] and Merrie Melodies cartoons for Warner Bros., including [[Bugs Bunny]], [[Daffy Duck]], Tweety and Sylvester the Cat. He began in radio comedy, including the Jack Benny program. Later he voiced Barney Rubble and Dino on [[The Flintstones]] for [[Hanna-Barbera]] and provided vocal effects for [[Tom and Jerry]] shorts from 1963 to 1967. His novelty records "I Tawt I Taw a Puddy Tat" and "The Woody Woodpecker Song" each sold over two million copies.

=== KEY: Mel_Brooks_5fa4f6
HEADWORD: Brooks, Mel
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Carl Reiner | David Lynch
ENTRY:
American filmmaker, actor and comedian, one of the few people to have won the EGOT awards (Emmy, Grammy, Oscar and Tony). Brooks began as a writer for Sid Caesar's *Your Show of Shows* and created the comedy sketch *The 2000 Year Old Man* with [[Carl Reiner]], which earned a Grammy for Best Comedy Album. He won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay for *The Producers* (1967). Comedies such as *Blazing Saddles* and *Young Frankenstein* (both 1974) followed, and his Broadway musical adaptation of *The Producers* won 12 Tony Awards. Through his company Brooksfilms, he produced [[David Lynch]]'s *The Elephant Man* (1980).

=== KEY: Mel_Gibson_d80641
HEADWORD: Gibson, Mel
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Sydney
ENTRY:
American actor and filmmaker known for the action series *Mad Max* and *Lethal Weapon*. Born in Peekskill, New York, he moved to [[Sydney]], Australia, at the age of 12, and won a Best Actor award from the Australian Film Institute for *Gallipoli* (1981). He directed and starred in *Braveheart* (1995), which won Academy Awards for Best Director and Best Picture. His direction of *The Passion of the Christ* (2004) was financially successful and highly controversial. After allegations of marital violence and hateful statements became public, his popularity in Hollywood declined. His directorial comeback, *Hacksaw Ridge* (2016), won two Academy Awards.
