ENTRY:
English actor, comedian, and television host. Corden co-wrote and starred in the [[BBC]] [[Sitcom|sitcom]] *Gavin & Stacey*, for which he won a BAFTA Television Award. He hosted *The Late Late Show with James Corden* on [[CBS]] from 2015 to 2023, and he created the Carpool Karaoke sketch in 2011. On Broadway he won a Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play for *One Man, Two Guvnors*. Corden has won 12 Primetime Emmy Awards from 29 nominations and was appointed an Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) in 2015 for services to drama.

=== KEY: James_Dean_97f3d6
HEADWORD: Dean, James
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Time (magazine)
ENTRY:
American actor. He appeared in just three major films, *East of Eden* (1955), *Rebel Without a Cause* (1955), and *Giant* (1956), all preserved in the National Film Registry. He received posthumous Academy Award nominations for Best Actor for *East of Eden* and *Giant*, making him the only actor with two posthumous acting nominations. In 1999 the American Film Institute ranked him 18th among the greatest male film stars of Golden Age Hollywood. [[Time (magazine)|Time]] named him one of the "All-Time Most Influential Fashion Icons" in 2012. He was killed in a car accident, and his image came to symbolize youthful rebellion.

=== KEY: James_Earl_Jones_3faee9
HEADWORD: Jones, James Earl
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: Darth Vader | Korean War | Stanley Kubrick
ENTRY:
American actor, known for his deep voice and as a pioneer for Black actors. He is one of the few performers to have won an Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, and Tony. He won two Tony Awards for Best Actor in a Play, for *The Great White Hope* and *Fences*, and acted in Shakespeare in the Park productions of *Hamlet* and *King Lear*. He served as an officer in the U.S. Army during the [[Korean War]]. His film debut was in [[Stanley Kubrick]]'s *Dr. Strangelove* (1964). He voiced [[Darth Vader]] in *Star Wars* and Mufasa in *The Lion King* (1994).

=== KEY: James_Earl_Ray_2cc7d6
HEADWORD: Ray, James Earl
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: George Wallace | Hepatitis C | London | Martin Luther King Jr.
ENTRY:
American criminal convicted of assassinating [[Martin Luther King Jr.]] in Memphis on April 4, 1968. After fleeing, he was captured in [[London]]. He pleaded guilty in 1969 to avoid a possible death sentence and received 99 years in prison, though he later tried to withdraw the plea. Ray supported segregationist [[George Wallace]] in 1968. A 1999 Memphis civil trial jury concluded that King was the victim of a conspiracy. The King family has said it believes Ray was innocent, a view the U.S. Department of Justice disputed in 2000. He died of liver failure caused by [[Hepatitis C|hepatitis C]] after serving twenty-nine years in prison.

=== KEY: James_Gandolfini_00239c
HEADWORD: Gandolfini, James
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: HBO
ENTRY:
American actor. He was best known for playing Tony Soprano, an Italian-American mafia boss, in the [[HBO]] series *The Sopranos* (1999–2007), a role regarded as one of the most influential performances in television history. The role won him three Primetime Emmy Awards, five Actor Awards, and a Golden Globe. His films include *True Romance* (1993), *Get Shorty* (1995), and *Zero Dark Thirty* (2012). He earned an Actor Award nomination for the romantic comedy *Enough Said* (2013). On Broadway he appeared in *A Streetcar Named Desire* and was nominated for a Tony Award for *God of Carnage*.
