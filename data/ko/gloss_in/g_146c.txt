=== KEY: White_dwarf_824784
HEADWORD: White dwarf
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Black hole | Heat | Neutron star | Nuclear fusion | Sirius | Solar mass | Sun
ENTRY:
Very dense stellar remnant, with a mass comparable to the [[Sun]] packed into an Earth-sized volume. A white dwarf shines from residual [[Heat|heat]] and slow crystallization, not from [[Nuclear fusion|nuclear fusion]]. Stars like the Sun, too light to collapse into a [[Neutron star|neutron star]] or [[Black hole|black hole]], are expected to become white dwarfs. The Chandrasekhar limit, about 1.44 times the mass of the Sun, is the maximum mass for a non-rotating white dwarf; observed masses are largely between 0.5 and 0.7 [[Solar mass|solar masses]]. The nearest known is [[Sirius]] B, 8.6 light years away. The first recognized, 40 Eridani B, was identified as white in 1910, and the name was coined in 1922.

=== KEY: White_hole_cd27ea
HEADWORD: White hole
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Black hole | General relativity | Spacetime | Stephen Hawking
ENTRY:
Hypothetical region of [[Spacetime|spacetime]] in [[General relativity|general relativity]] that cannot be entered from outside, although matter, energy, light and information can escape from it. It is the opposite of a [[Black hole|black hole]]. White holes appear in the theory of eternal black holes, as a region in the past of a black hole, but no physical process has been observed that could form one. [[Stephen Hawking|Stephen Hawking]] argued that a black hole in thermal equilibrium would time-reverse into a white hole, and others have proposed that supermassive black holes could spawn supermassive white holes.

=== KEY: White_people_2fd3ca
HEADWORD: White people
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Europe | Race (human categorization) | Slavery
ENTRY:
Racial classification most often applied to people of [[Europe|European]] origin, and in a broader sense to anyone with light skin. The modern concept of a unified [[Race (human categorization)|White race]] entered the major European languages in the late 17th century, in the context of race-based [[Slavery|slavery]]. Before then, Europeans defined their identity by religion, ancestry, ethnicity or nationality. Greco-Roman ethnography occasionally described populations as white by skin color, but these societies had no concept of whiteness as a race. Contemporary anthropologists and other scientists regard a unified White race as a social construct with no scientific basis.

=== KEY: White_rhinoceros_53ef4c
HEADWORD: White rhinoceros
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Grazing | Kenya | Rhinoceros
ENTRY:
Largest living species of [[Rhinoceros|rhinoceros]], with a wide mouth adapted for [[Grazing|grazing]] and the most social of all rhinoceros species. The southern subspecies numbered an estimated 17,464 in the wild as of the end of 2023. The northern subspecies is critically endangered: as of 2018 only two were confirmed alive, both females held in captivity at Ol Pejeta. The last known male, Sudan, died in [[Kenya]] in March 2018. White rhinoceros can run up to 40 mph (64 km/h) in short bursts.

=== KEY: White_supremacy_295703
HEADWORD: White supremacy
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Apartheid | Atlantic slave trade | Ku Klux Klan | Neo-Nazism | Scramble for Africa | White people
ENTRY:
Belief that [[White people|white people]] are superior to people of other races, and the ideology that maintains white power and privilege. It has roots in the now-discredited doctrine of scientific racism and justified European colonialism. Its structures included the [[Atlantic slave trade|Atlantic slave trade]], the [[Scramble for Africa]], Jim Crow laws and [[Apartheid|apartheid]] in South Africa. Contemporary forms include white nationalism and [[Neo-Nazism|neo-Nazism]], and in the United States it is associated with the [[Ku Klux Klan]]. In critical race theory the term also describes a social system in which white people hold structural advantages despite formal legal equality.

=== KEY: White_tailed_deer_4b0f76
HEADWORD: White-tailed deer
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Andes | Cougar | Deer | Herbivore | Rocky Mountains | Ungulate | Yukon
ENTRY:
Medium-sized [[Deer|deer]] native to North and South America, also called the whitetail or Virginia deer. It is the most widely distributed mainland [[Ungulate|ungulate]] [[Herbivore|herbivore]] in the Americas and, with its predator the [[Cougar|mountain lion]], one of the most widely distributed terrestrial mammal species. It inhabits everything from arid grasslands to the Amazon and Orinoco basins and the high [[Andes]]. In North America it is common east and south of the [[Rocky Mountains]] and extends north to the [[Yukon]]. Texas is home to by far the most, with an estimated population of 5.3 million.

=== KEY: White_tiger_303dfc
HEADWORD: White tiger
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Bengal tiger | Fertility | Immune system | Inbreeding | Tiger
ENTRY:
Leucistic color morph of the [[Tiger|tiger]], typically the [[Bengal tiger]], with black stripes on otherwise white or near-white fur and blue eyes. Its pale coat results from a lack of red and yellow pheomelanin pigments. Natural occurrences are occasionally reported in India, but the last one seen in the wild was reportedly shot in 1958. Most white tigers result from intentional [[Inbreeding|inbreeding]] for entertainment, a practice the Association of Zoos and Aquariums bans for accredited institutions. Inbreeding causes health problems, including reduced [[Fertility|fertility]], weakened [[Immune system|immune systems]] and shortened lifespans.

=== KEY: Whitefly_288162
HEADWORD: Whitefly
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Hemiptera | Leaf | Pesticide
ENTRY:
[[Hemiptera|Hemipteran]] insects of the family Aleyrodidae, which typically feed on the undersides of plant [[Leaf|leaves]]. More than 1550 species have been described. Adults are 1 mm to 2 mm long, and many species are dusted with a floury wax, which gave the family its name. Their small size lets them pass through fine screens that keep out their natural enemies, so unchecked greenhouse populations can rapidly become overwhelming. Whiteflies excrete honeydew, which promotes sooty mold, and vector more than 200 plant viruses. Control relies on biological agents such as the parasitoid *Encarsia formosa*, along with cultural practices and [[Pesticide|pesticides]], though some species resist insecticides.

=== KEY: Whitehorse_99707c
HEADWORD: Whitehorse
PERSON: no
LINK TARGETS: Yukon | Yukon River
ENTRY:
Capital and largest city of [[Yukon]], Canada, on both shores of the [[Yukon River]] in southern Yukon. It was incorporated in 1950 and is named after the White Horse Rapids, which resemble a white horse's mane. First Nations used the area for several thousand years. After gold was discovered in the Klondike in August 1896, prospectors arrived by steamship, and the White Pass and Yukon Route railway, completed in 1900, linked the city to Skagway. The 2021 census recorded 28,201 residents, about 70 percent of the territory's population.

=== KEY: Whitey_Bulger_ccf126
HEADWORD: Bulger, Whitey
PERSON: yes
LINK TARGETS: American Mafia | Boston | Organized crime | Osama bin Laden
ENTRY:
American [[Organized crime|organized crime]] boss who led the Winter Hill Gang, an Irish mob group near [[Boston]]. The FBI said he informed on Italian-American [[American Mafia|Mafia]] rivals from 1975, which he denied. Tipped off about a racketeering indictment, he fled in 1994 and was at large for sixteen years, ranked second on the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list behind [[Osama bin Laden]]. He was captured in California in 2011. Convicted in 2013 of racketeering and involvement in eleven murders, he received two consecutive life sentences. Fellow inmates beat him to death within hours of transfer to a West Virginia prison.
