Liquid, cream, or semi-solid food served on or used in preparing other foods. Most sauces are not eaten alone; they add flavor, texture, and visual appeal. The word comes from French, probably from post-classical Latin *salsa*, derived from the classical *salsus*, meaning salted. Possibly the oldest recorded European sauce is garum, a fish sauce used by the ancient Romans. The Aztecs are thought to have made one of the oldest sauces, hot sauce from [[Chili pepper|chili peppers]] and water, in roughly 7000 BCE. Sauces may be served cold, like [[Mayonnaise]], or cooked and served warm. Composed sauces, important in classical [[French cuisine]], are made in the kitchen, and the five mother sauces were refined by Georges Auguste Escoffier in his 1903 cookbook *Le Guide Culinaire*. In Asian cooking, sauces are often built into a dish, as in curries, or used as a [[Condiment|condiment]], such as [[Soy sauce|soy sauce]].

Kingdom, West Asia, occupying the bulk of the [[Arabian Peninsula]] (pop., 2024: 36,544,431). Its land area of about 2,149,690 sq km makes it the fifth-largest country in Asia and the twelfth-largest in the world. It borders the Red Sea to the west and the Persian Gulf to the east. [[Riyadh]] is the capital and largest city; other major centers include Jeddah and the two holiest cities in Islam, [[Mecca]] and [[Medina]].

Islam emerged in the territory in the early 7th century, and after the death of Muhammad in 632 his followers expanded Muslim rule into North Africa, Central and South Asia, and Iberia.

A founding member of [[OPEC]], the UN, and the Arab League, it is the only Arab member of the G20 and has the largest economy in the Middle East. Foreign-born residents make up roughly 40% of the population.

Heated room or building for dry or wet heat sessions, or an establishment with such facilities. Bathers sweat in high heat, and steam is produced when water is thrown on hot stones. The word is an ancient Finnish term for both the traditional Finnish bath and the bathhouse. The first Finnish smoke saunas (savusauna) were heated for six to eight hours by burning wood under a pile of stones called a kiuas, and could yield heat for up to 12 hours. Extinct clade of saurischian [[Dinosaur|dinosaurs]] with very long necks and tails. Sauropods had small heads and four thick, pillar-like legs, and the group includes the largest animals ever to live on land. The name, coined by Othniel Charles Marsh in 1878, means "lizard foot" in Greek. The oldest unequivocal sauropods date from the [[Jurassic|Early Jurassic]]. By the Late Jurassic, about 150 million years ago, they were widespread, especially diplodocids and brachiosaurids. By the Late Cretaceous the titanosaurs had replaced all other sauropods, with a near-global distribution, but like all other non-avian dinosaurs they died out in the [[Cretaceous–Paleogene extinction event]]. Fossils have been found on every continent. Complete skeletons are extremely rare, and many of the largest species are known only from isolated bones.

Mixed woodland-grassland [[Biome|biome]] and ecosystem in which trees are spaced so that the canopy does not close. The open canopy lets enough light reach the ground to support an unbroken layer of grasses. Savannas take four forms: savanna woodland, tree savanna, shrub savanna, and grass savanna. Despite their open look, savannas can have high tree densities; South American cerrado ranges from 800 to 3,300 trees per [[Hectare|hectare]], and Guinean savanna has 129 trees per hectare. Unlike the [[Prairie|prairies]] of North America and [[Steppe|steppes]] of Eurasia, they lack cold winters. The word comes from Spanish *sabana*, a loanword from Taíno meaning treeless grassland.

[[Tool]] consisting of a tough blade, wire, or chain with a hard toothed edge, used to cut through material, most often [[Wood|wood]]. Many hand saws and mechanical saws exist, with several terms for toothed and abrasive types. Range of physical processes in which moving particles or radiation are deflected from a straight trajectory, or blocked, by localized non-uniformities in the medium they pass through. Mental disorder characterized by [[Hallucination|hallucinations]] (typically hearing voices), [[Delusion|delusions]], disorganized thinking or behavior, and flat or inappropriate affect.
