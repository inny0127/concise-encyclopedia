Division of fungi that, with [[Ascomycota]], forms the subkingdom Dikarya, often called the higher fungi. Members, called basidiomycetes, reproduce sexually on club-shaped basidia that usually bear four external spores called basidiospores. The division includes agarics, puffballs, bracket fungi, boletes, chanterelles, smuts and rusts, as well as [[Mushroom|mushrooms]] and [[Yeast|yeasts]] such as Cryptococcus, the human pathogenic yeast. Traditionally it was divided into two classes, now obsolete: the homobasidiomycetes, including true mushrooms, and the heterobasidiomycetes, including the jelly, rust and smut fungi.

[[Weaving|Weaving]] or sewing of pliable materials, such as [[Fiber|fiber]], [[Bark (botany)|bark]] or [[Willow|willow]], into objects such as baskets, mats and mesh bags, or even furniture. Basketry is one of the most widespread crafts in human history, although natural materials decay, so evidence is limited. The most common evidence is weave imprints on fragments of [[Pottery|clay pots]]. [[Adolf Hitler]] planned the invasion Operation Sea Lion, which was postponed and then canceled after the Luftwaffe failed to gain air superiority. Germany's failure to destroy Britain's air defenses was its first major defeat in the war.

Engagement of the Second Punic War, fought on 2 August 216 BC near the village of Cannae in [[Apulia]], southeast Italy. [[Hannibal]] and the Carthaginian army surrounded and practically annihilated a larger Roman and Italian army, using the double envelopment tactic.
