In 1954 the sociologist Edward Shils used the term for anti-elite trends in U.S. society during the rise of [[McCarthyism]]. One later definition describes populism as a thin-centered ideology that divides society into a pure people and a corrupt elite, which can attach to left-wing or right-wing ideologies. Scholars disagree about its effects on liberal democracy.
