Non-profit media organization publishing leaked documents. Founded in 2006 by [[Julian Assange]], it says it has released more than ten million documents. It is the ancestor of the [[Domestic turkey|domestic turkey]], which derives from a southern Mexican subspecies. Unplanned, uncontrolled fire in an area of combustible vegetation. Some natural forest ecosystems depend on wildfire, and many plant species rely on fire for growth and reproduction. Forest managers use prescribed burns to reduce fire risk, although controlled burns can escape containment. Ignition comes from natural causes such as [[Lightning|lightning]], volcanic eruptions and spontaneous combustion, or from human activity, which is responsible for over 85% of wildfires in the United States. Severe fires often follow wet periods that build fuel and then [[Drought|drought]] and heat, and [[Climate change|climate change]] has intensified these cycles. Human practices have caused a doubling in land area burned compared with natural levels. Wildfire releases [[Carbon dioxide|carbon dioxide]], which adds to the greenhouse effect and creates a climate feedback.
