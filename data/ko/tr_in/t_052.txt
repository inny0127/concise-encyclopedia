Region of southern [[Italy]], a peninsula between the [[Ionian Sea]] and the Tyrrhenian Sea. It has a population of 1,827,571 across 15,221.90 sq km, and Catanzaro is its capital. Greek colonies were founded on its coast from the 8th century BC, and the region was the heart of *Magna Graecia*. After the Gothic War it was a [[Byzantine Empire|Byzantine]] dominion for five centuries, and the Byzantines made it the main [[Silk|silk]] production area in Europe. Three historical ethnolinguistic minorities remain, speaking Calabrian Greek, Albanian and Occitan. It has three national parks, including Pollino, the largest in Italy.

Carbonate mineral and the most stable form of [[Calcium carbonate]] (CaCO₃). It is very common, particularly as a component of [[Limestone|limestone]], and defines hardness 3 on the [[Mohs scale]]. Calcite is usually white or colorless, though impurities can tint it gray, red, orange or other colors. Large calcite crystals are used in optical equipment. Its specific gravity is 2.71. The related mineral aragonite changes to calcite over days or less at temperatures above 300 °C.

ENTRY:
Reactive [[Alkaline earth metal|alkaline earth metal]], chemical element of atomic number 20 (symbol Ca). Calcium is the fifth most abundant element in Earth's crust and the third most abundant metal, after [[Iron]] and [[Aluminium]]. Its name comes from the Latin *calx* ("lime"), obtained from heating [[Limestone]]. Its most common compound on Earth is [[Calcium carbonate]], found in limestone and the fossils of early sea life. [[Humphry Davy]] isolated pure calcium in 1808 by electrolysis of its oxide and named the element. The metal melts at 842 °C. Calcium compounds are used in cement, paper, soaps and food supplements, and small amounts of the metal serve as an alloy in [[Steelmaking|steelmaking]]. Calcium is the most abundant metal in the human body, where its ions play a vital role in signal transduction, [[Neurotransmitter|neurotransmitter]] release, muscle contraction and bone formation.

Common compound (CaCO₃) found in rocks as the minerals [[Calcite|calcite]] and aragonite. It occurs in [[Chalk|chalk]] and [[Limestone|limestone]], eggshells, shells, skeletons and [[Pearl|pearls]]. Limescale, produced when calcium ions in hard water react with carbonate ions, is also calcium carbonate. In the carbon cycle it sequesters large amounts of carbon in geological formations, and acidic water entering a deposit can form caves with stalactites and stalagmites. Heating calcium carbonate releases carbon dioxide and leaves [[Calcium oxide|calcium oxide]]. It is used as agricultural lime to correct soil chemistry, as a calcium supplement or antacid, and as a filler in [[Paper|paper]] and plastic. Excess intake can cause hypercalcemia and digestive problems.

Inorganic salt (CaCl₂), a white crystalline solid that is highly soluble in water. It is made by neutralizing [[Hydrochloric acid]] with [[Calcium hydroxide]]. Its hydrates are used mainly for de-icing and dust control, and solutions can prevent freezing at temperatures as low as −52 °C (−62 °F). On dirt roads, a concentrated solution keeps a liquid layer that suppresses dust, reducing the need for grading by as much as 50% and fill-in materials by as much as 80%. The anhydrous salt is hygroscopic and deliquescent, so it serves as a desiccant. In food, it is used as a firming agent in canned vegetables, such as tomatoes and cucumber pickles, and in firming soybean curds into [[Tofu]]. It was apparently discovered in the 15th century but was not studied properly until the 18th.

Inorganic compound, Ca(OH)₂, traditionally called slaked lime. It is produced when [[Calcium oxide|quicklime]] is mixed with water and forms a colorless crystal or white powder. Approximately 125 million tons are produced worldwide each year. Its saturated solution is called limewater, which turns milky when carbon dioxide is passed through it. Calcium hydroxide is used to prepare lime mortar, as a flocculant in water and [[Sewage treatment|sewage treatment]], and as a neutralizing agent in municipal wastewater treatment.
