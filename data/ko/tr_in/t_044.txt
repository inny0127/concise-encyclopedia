ENTRY:
Wingless, blood-feeding [[Louse|louse]] that exclusively infests humans. Also called the clothing louse, it lives mainly in the seams of clothing and feeds on blood several times a day. Body lice cannot jump or fly, and spread by direct contact or through infested clothing and bedding. They transmit epidemic typhus, trench fever, and louse-borne relapsing fever. These pathogens have caused devastating outbreaks, including millions of deaths in World Wars I and II. Infestations occur where overcrowding, poor hygiene, and a lack of clean clothing prevail, such as in prisons, refugee camps, and homeless populations. Control relies on washing clothes and bedding in hot water of at least 54°C (130°F) and on delousing treatments.

  Quetelet Index = 케틀레 지수

ENTRY:
Value (BMI) derived from a person's mass and height, used to categorize body weight. It is calculated as body mass in [[Kilogram|kilograms]] divided by the square of body height in square meters. Adult categories are underweight (under 18.5), normal weight (18.5 to 24.9), overweight (25 to 29.9), and [[Obesity|obese]] (30 or more). Originally called the Quetelet Index, it was devised in the 1830s by Adolphe Quetelet to describe populations rather than individuals. BMI does not distinguish lean mass from fat mass, so it may misclassify muscular athletes as overweight. Values under 20 and over 25 have been associated with higher all-cause mortality.

American rock band formed in Sayreville, [[New Jersey]], in 1983. Its third album, *Slippery When Wet* (1986), produced two number-one singles, "You Give Love a Bad Name" and "Livin' on a Prayer". The band has sold more than 130 million records worldwide and was inducted into the [[Rock and Roll Hall of Fame]] in 2018. Richie Sambora left in 2013, and the band later released *This House Is Not for Sale* (2016) without him. Its album *2020* was reworked to include songs inspired by the [[COVID-19 pandemic]] and George Floyd protests.

ENTRY:
Caribbean island and special municipality of the [[Netherlands]] (pop., 2025: 26,552). Bonaire lies about 80 km off the coast of Venezuela and forms one of the [[ABC islands]] with [[Aruba]] and [[Curaçao]]. Its arid climate and location outside the Main Development Region for [[Tropical cyclone|tropical cyclones]] attract visitors. Its reefs are popular for snorkeling and scuba diving and are protected by the Bonaire National Marine Park. The island became a special municipality in 2010, when the Netherlands Antilles was dissolved.

American [[Western (genre)|Western]] television series broadcast on [[NBC]] from 1959 to 1973. Set in the 1860s, it follows the wealthy Cartwright family, who run the fictional Ponderosa Ranch near Virginia City, [[Nevada]], bordering [[Lake Tahoe]]. The series starred Lorne Greene, Pernell Roberts, Dan Blocker and [[Michael Landon]] over 14 seasons and 431 episodes. It was NBC's longest-running Western and the second-longest-running Western on American network television, behind [[CBS]]'s *Gunsmoke*. In 2002, TV Guide ranked it No. 43 on its list of the 50 Greatest TV Shows of All Time.
