Smallest functional unit of striated [[Skeletal muscle|muscle]] tissue, the repeating segment between two Z-lines in a myofibril. Sarcomeres give skeletal and cardiac muscle its striated appearance, but the myofibrils of smooth muscle are not arranged into sarcomeres.

Clade of [[Vertebrate|vertebrate]] animals comprising the lobe-finned fishes and all four-limbed land animals, or [[Tetrapod|tetrapods]]. In contrast to the [[Actinopterygii]], whose fins are supported only by bony spines, lobe-finned fish have fleshy, muscular lobes in their fins supported by internal bones. Sarcopterygii emerged in the Silurian and early Devonian periods as entirely aquatic species. Their lobed fins evolved into limbs, and the foregut evolved into air-breathing lungs, enabling tetrapods to colonize land. Most lobe-finned fish are extinct; during the Carboniferous and [[Permian]] periods they were dominant predators of freshwater ecosystems. Receivers determine their location to within a few centimeters to meters using time signals transmitted by [[Radio|radio]] from satellites. The same signals allow receivers to calculate local time to high precision. Global coverage is generally achieved by a constellation of 18–30 medium Earth orbit satellites, at an altitude of about 20,000 kilometers (12,000 miles). Satellite navigation operates independently of telephone or internet reception.

Sixth planet from the Sun and second largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter. A [[Gas giant|gas giant]] with an average radius about 9 times that of Earth, it has an eighth of Earth's average density but is over 95 times more massive. It orbits the Sun at 9.59 AU (1,434 million km) in 29.45 years, with a day of about 10.7 hours.

Saturn is composed predominantly of hydrogen and helium and lacks a definite surface. Its interior is thought to include a diffuse rocky and icy core, a deep layer of [[Metallic hydrogen|metallic hydrogen]], a layer of liquid hydrogen and helium, and an outer gas layer. Its average density, 0.69 g/cm³, makes it the only planet less dense than water. It radiates 2.5 times more energy than it receives from the Sun. The main system spans about 282,000 km, with divisions such as the 4,700-km-wide Cassini Division. At least 293 moons orbit Saturn, 63 of them officially named. [[Titan (moon)|Titan]], the largest, is the second largest moon in the Solar System, larger than Mercury, and the only moon with a substantial atmosphere. [[Enceladus]] ejects water plumes that indicate a subsurface ocean.

NASA's Pioneer 11 flew past in 1979, followed by [[Voyager 1]] and Voyager 2 in the early 1980s and the Cassini–Huygens mission from 2004 to 2017, which landed the Huygens probe on Titan.
