Numerical label assigned to a device on a computer network that uses the [[Internet Protocol]]. Ancient Chinese divination text, also called *Book of Changes*, and among the oldest Chinese classics. Originally a divination manual of the [[Zhou dynasty|Western Zhou period]], it was expanded during the Warring States and early imperial periods with philosophical commentaries known as the Ten Wings. After becoming part of the Chinese Five Classics in the 2nd century BC, it was the basis of divination for centuries across the Far East. Divination uses bundles of yarrow stalks to produce sets of six numbers from 6 to 9, and each of the 64 possible sets corresponds to a hexagram. Commentators have read the text as moral guidance informed by [[Confucianism]], [[Taoism]] and [[Buddhism]], and the hexagrams have been linked to [[Yin and yang|yin and yang]] and other cosmological ideas.

ENTRY:
Period of long-term cooling of Earth's surface and atmosphere, marked by prolonged expansion of continental [[Glacier|glaciers]] and polar ice caps. Ice ages contain colder glacial periods alternating with warmer interglacials. Earth is in the Late Cenozoic Ice Age, which began 34 million years ago, and the Quaternary glaciation began 2.58 million years ago. The most recent glacial period, the [[Last Glacial Period]], was followed by the warm [[Holocene]], which began 11,700 years ago. The [[Last Glacial Maximum]] peaked between 26,000 and 20,000 years ago. Scientific understanding developed slowly: in 1795 [[James Hutton]] explained erratic boulders in the Alps by the action of glaciers, and in 1824 Jens Esmark proposed that changes in climate, linked to changes in Earth's orbit, caused a sequence of ice ages.

ENTRY:
Frozen dessert typically made from [[Milk|milk]] or [[Cream|cream]], sweetened and flavored with a spice such as [[Vanilla|vanilla]] or with fruit. The mixture is cooled below the freezing point of water and stirred to incorporate air and prevent large ice crystals from forming. It is served in dishes or cones, or in desserts such as sundaes, milkshakes and Baked Alaska. *Gelato*, the Italian word for ice cream, is often used in English. The history likely began in ancient Persia around 550 BC, when Persian techniques for storing ice led to early frozen desserts. In many countries the name is legally defined: in the United States and the United Kingdom, the term applies only to products meeting a specification based largely on butterfat from cream.

ENTRY:
Team sport played on ice skates by two teams of six players on a rink. Teams score by shooting a vulcanized rubber puck into the opponent's net with their sticks, and each goal is worth one point. The team with more goals after three 20-minute periods wins, and ties are broken in overtime or a shootout. The modern sport was developed in Canada, most notably in [[Montreal]], where the first indoor game was played on March 3, 1875. It draws influence from [[Field hockey]] and from shinty. The Stanley Cup, first awarded in 1893, became the championship trophy of the [[National Hockey League|NHL]]. Ice hockey was first played at the Olympics at the 1920 Summer Games and is now a mainstay of the Winter Games. In 1994 it was officially recognized as Canada's national winter sport.

ENTRY:
Mass of glacial ice covering more than 50,000 sq km of land, also called a continental glacier. Ice sheets are the largest glacial bodies on Earth. The two that exist today are the [[Antarctic ice sheet]], covering 14 million sq km, and the [[Greenland ice sheet]], covering 1.7 million sq km. Ice sheets flow outward from central domes, moving faster in ice streams where meltwater lubricates the base. At the maximum of the [[Last Glacial Period]], the Laurentide and Cordilleran ice sheets merged with the Greenland ice sheet to cover much of North America. The ongoing retreat of the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets plays a major role in [[Climate change]] and sea level rise.
