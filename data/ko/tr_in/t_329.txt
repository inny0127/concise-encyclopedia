Its standardized form, Filipino, is the national language and one of the country's two official languages, alongside English. Tagalog is a Central Philippine language, closely related to the Bikol and Visayan languages and more distantly to Malay and Hawaiian. It follows the trigger system of morphosyntactic alignment common among Philippine languages and has nine basic parts of speech. The first complete book in Tagalog, the *Doctrina Christiana*, was printed in 1593 in both Baybayin script and a Latin transcription. During the 333 years of Spanish rule, clergymen wrote grammars and dictionaries of the language.

Genus of about 50 species of annual or perennial, mostly herbaceous plants in the family [[Asteraceae|Asteraceae]], many known as marigolds. Native to Central and Southern [[Mexico|Mexico]] and several other [[Latin America|Latin American]] countries, they were originally called *cempōhualxōchitl* by the Nahua people. [[Carl Linnaeus|Carl Linnaeus]] formally named the genus in 1753. Most species have pinnate green leaves and blooms in golden, orange, yellow, and white colors. Largest island of the Windward group of the Society Islands in [[French Polynesia]] (pop., 2017: 189,517). It is made up of two roughly circular land masses, Tahiti Nui and Tahiti Iti, joined by an [[Isthmus]]; both are eroded remnants of extinct [[Shield volcano|shield volcanoes]]. The island lies in the central [[Pacific Ocean]], and its beaches and coral reefs support tourism and fishing. The capital of French Polynesia, Papeʻete, is on the island's northwest coast. Polynesians settled the island between 900 and 1100 CE. France annexed it in 1880, and French is the sole official language, though Tahitian is widely spoken.

Special municipality in central [[Taiwan]] and the island's second-largest city, with more than 2.86 million residents. Located in the Taichung Basin, it grew from scattered hamlets of Taiwanese indigenous peoples who lived by cultivating [[Millet|millet]] and [[Taro|taro]]. The Qing renamed the settlement Taiwan-fu between 1887 and 1894, when it was planned as the provincial capital, but the capital was moved to [[Taipei]]. The city is home to the National Museum of Natural Science, the National Taichung Theater and the historic Taichung Park.

ENTRY:
Biome of [[Conifer|coniferous]] forests dominated by [[Pine|pines]], [[Spruce|spruces]] and larches, also called boreal forest. It is the world's largest land biome, covering 17 million square kilometers (6.6 million square miles), or 11.5% of Earth's land area. In North America it covers most of inland Canada and Alaska. In Eurasia it extends across Sweden, Finland, much of Russia including most of [[Siberia]], and parts of northern Mongolia and Japan's Hokkaido. The taiga in its current form has existed only for the last 12,000 years, since the start of the [[Holocene]] epoch. Apart from the permanent ice caps and [[Tundra]], it has the lowest annual average temperatures of any terrestrial biome, and winters below freezing last 5–7 months. [[Climate change]] threatens it, and how the carbon dioxide it absorbs or emits should be counted in carbon accounting is controversial.
