Connection between bones, ossicles or other hard structures of the [[Skeleton|skeletal system]] that permits movement. Joints are classified structurally, by the binding tissue that connects the bones, and functionally, by the degree of movement they allow. Short humorous story, usually with dialogue, that ends in a punch line. The linguist Robert Hetzron defined a joke as a short humorous piece of oral literature in which the funniness culminates in the final sentence. Jokes benefit from brevity, and the shaggy dog story is an anti-joke that deliberately withholds a punch line. Jokes are passed along anonymously, in private conversation, in scripted entertainment and, more recently, online. French mathematician and physicist. Born in Auxerre, the son of a tailor, Fourier was orphaned at nine and educated by the Benedictines. He was imprisoned briefly during the Terror, then taught at the École Polytechnique. He accompanied Napoleon to Egypt in 1798 as scientific adviser and was later appointed prefect of the Isère department at Grenoble, where he began experiments on the propagation of heat. His 1822 treatise *Théorie analytique de la chaleur* showed that arbitrary functions could be represented as infinite sums of sine and cosine terms.
