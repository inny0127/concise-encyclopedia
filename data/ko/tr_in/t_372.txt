Inland province of southwestern China, bordering [[Myanmar]], [[Vietnam]] and [[Laos]] (pop., 2020: 47,209,277). Its capital is [[Kunming]]. The province spans about 394,000 sq km, with high mountains in the northwest and most of its population in the east. It has the largest diversity of plant life in China, with perhaps 17,000 of the country's roughly 30,000 vascular plant species. Its reserves of aluminum, lead, zinc and tin are the largest in China, and it has major copper and nickel reserves. Yunnan is identified as the birthplace of [[Tea|tea]] and as the region of origin of the plant genus [[Cannabis]].

Highland region of southwest [[China]], spread mainly over [[Yunnan]] and Guizhou provinces. Under its strictest definition it stretches from the Red River Fault in Yunnan to the Wuling Mountains in Hunan, covering most of eastern Yunnan and Guizhou. Its southwest is a true plateau with flatter highlands, while the northeast is mountainous, with rolling hills, gorges and karst topography.
