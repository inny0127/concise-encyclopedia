Spiny [[Mammal|mammal]] of the subfamily Erinaceinae, with 17 species in five genera. Native to Europe, Asia and Africa, hedgehogs were introduced to New Zealand, and none are native to Australia or the Americas. They are mostly nocturnal omnivores, eating insects, [[Snail|snails]], frogs, snakes and eggs. Their spines are hollow hairs stiffened with keratin, and they roll into a tight ball when threatened. Many hedgehogs hibernate in underground dens. Hedgehogs share distant ancestry with [[Shrew|shrews]] and have changed little in the last 15 million years. They are sensitive to stress from humans, and wildlife clinics recommend keeping captured animals in a dark, ventilated box with minimal noise.

ENTRY:
Family of philosophical views that give priority to [[Pleasure|pleasure]]. Psychological hedonism holds that all human behavior aims at maximizing pleasure and minimizing pain, and axiological hedonism holds that pleasure is the sole source of intrinsic value. Ethical hedonism holds that people have a moral duty to pursue pleasure. Utilitarian versions seek the greatest happiness for everyone, while egoistic versions pursue one's own pleasure. Discussed by the Cyrenaics and [[Epicurus]] in ancient Greece, hedonism became central to modern thought with the rise of [[Utilitarianism]]. Jeremy Bentham proposed a calculus to measure pleasures by intensity and duration. Critics argue that it overlooks goods such as knowledge and virtue.

German physicist. Hertz was the first to conclusively prove the existence of the electromagnetic waves predicted by [[James Clerk Maxwell]]'s equations. Common iron oxide mineral, Fe₂O₃, found in rocks and soils. It is mined as an important [[Iron|iron]] ore and is harder than pure iron but much more brittle. [[Iron]]-containing protein that carries [[Oxygen|oxygen]] in [[Red blood cell|red blood cells]]. It carries oxygen from the lungs or gills to the body's tissues, where it is released for [[Respiration (physiology)|aerobic respiration]]. In mammals hemoglobin makes up about 96% of a red blood cell's dry weight, and it can bind and transport up to four oxygen molecules. Its oxygen-binding capacity of 1.34 mL of O₂ per gram increases the total blood oxygen capacity seventy-fold compared with dissolved oxygen in plasma alone. Vascular cushions in the anal canal that help control stool. When swollen or [[Inflammation|inflamed]], they become a disease. Internal hemorrhoids often cause painless, bright red bleeding during [[Defecation|defecation]], while external ones cause pain and swelling. Constipation, [[Diarrhea|diarrhea]] and long periods on the [[Toilet|toilet]] are believed to raise abdominal pressure and contribute, and hemorrhoids are more common in [[Pregnancy|pregnancy]].
