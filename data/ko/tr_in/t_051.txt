Forms of ownership vary by jurisdiction. A sole proprietorship is owned by one person, who has unlimited liability for the business's obligations and is personally taxed on all its income. The management theorist Peter Drucker defined the purpose of a business as creating a customer, with profit an essential condition for survival rather than the primary goal. Businesses have driven technological advance and raised living standards since the Industrial Revolution, though critics point to monopolistic behavior and environmental impacts, which prompt regulation.

Among patients, 81% have mild to moderate illness, 14% severe illness and 5% critical illness, such as [[Respiratory failure|respiratory failure]] or [[Shock (circulatory)|shock]]. Older people are at higher risk of severe disease, and some people have persistent symptoms, known as [[Long COVID]]. Transmission occurs when infectious particles are breathed in or contact the eyes, nose or mouth. Vaccines have been approved and distributed in many countries.

Family of [[DNA|DNA]] sequences found in the genomes of [[Prokaryote|prokaryotes]] such as [[Bacteria|bacteria]] and [[Archaea|archaea]]. Each sequence derives from a fragment of a bacteriophage that infected the organism, and it helps detect and destroy DNA from similar viruses, providing heritable immunity. CRISPR is found in approximately 50% of sequenced bacterial genomes and nearly 90% of sequenced archaea. The [[Enzyme|enzyme]] Cas9 uses CRISPR sequences as a guide to recognize and open specific DNA strands, forming the basis of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing. Emmanuelle Charpentier and Jennifer Doudna received the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing it.

ENTRY:
Style sheet language that specifies the presentation of documents written in [[HTML|HTML]], XML or similar markup languages. CSS is a cornerstone technology of the [[World Wide Web|World Wide Web]], alongside HTML and [[JavaScript|JavaScript]]. It separates content from presentation, covering layout, [[Color|colors]] and fonts, so that many pages can share one cached style sheet and the same markup can be presented on screen, in print, by speech or on Braille devices. Håkon Wium Lie proposed it in 1994 while working at [[CERN|CERN]]. CSS also has rules for alternative formatting on a [[Mobile device|mobile device]]. The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) maintains its specifications, and CSS Level 1 was published as a Recommendation in December 1996.
