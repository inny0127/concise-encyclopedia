Spy and infiltrator in pre-modern Japan, also called *shinobi*. Antecedents may have existed as early as the 12th century. Chemical element with symbol Nb and atomic number 41. Niobium is a light gray, crystalline [[Transition metal|transition metal]] that oxidizes very slowly in air, which makes it a hypoallergenic alternative to nickel in jewelry. Its name comes from Niobe, daughter of Tantalus, reflecting its similarity to [[Tantalum]]. Charles Hatchett reported it in 1801 as columbium, and Heinrich Rose named niobium in 1846. Polyatomic ion, NO₃⁻, found in [[Fertilizer|fertilizers]] and explosives. Salts containing the ion are called nitrates, and nearly all inorganic nitrates are soluble in water. In nature, [[Bacteria|nitrifying bacteria]] produce nitrates from ammonia or urea. ENTRY:
Chemical element with symbol N and atomic number 7, a [[Nonmetal|nonmetal]] and the lightest member of group 15 of the periodic table, the pnictogens. At standard temperature and pressure two atoms bond to form N₂, a colorless, odorless diatomic [[Gas|gas]] that makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere, the most abundant chemical species in air. It is estimated to be seventh in abundance in the [[Milky Way]] and the [[Solar System]].

The Scottish physician Daniel Rutherford is credited with discovering nitrogen in 1772, calling it noxious air; Carl Wilhelm Scheele and [[Henry Cavendish]] did so independently. [[Antoine Lavoisier]] proposed the name *azote*, from the Greek for "no life," because it is an asphyxiant; the name remains in many languages and in compounds such as hydrazine. The English name derives from *nitrogène*, coined in 1790 by Jean-Antoine Chaptal.

The extremely strong triple bond in N₂, the second strongest in any diatomic molecule after carbon monoxide, dominates nitrogen chemistry. It makes converting N₂ into useful compounds difficult, yet decomposing nitrogen compounds releases large amounts of energy. Elemental nitrogen is usually produced from air by pressure swing adsorption. About two-thirds is used as an inert gas, for example in food packaging, and much of the rest as [[Liquid nitrogen|liquid nitrogen]] in cryogenics. Important compounds include [[Ammonia|ammonia]], nitric acid, organic nitrates, and cyanides. Synthetic ammonia and nitrates are key fertilizers, and fertilizer nitrates are key pollutants in the eutrophication of water systems.

Nitrogen occurs in all organisms, mainly in amino acids, nucleic acids, and Adenosine triphosphate. It is about 3% of the human body by mass, the fourth most abundant element after oxygen, carbon, and hydrogen.

Statement that every continuous symmetry of the action of a physical system with conservative forces has a corresponding conservation law. [[Emmy Noether|Emmy Noether]] published it in 1918 as the first of two theorems. The action is the time integral of a Lagrangian function. Rotational symmetry implies conservation of angular momentum, time-translation symmetry implies conservation of energy, and spatial-translation symmetry implies conservation of linear momentum. The theorem is used in theoretical physics and the calculus of variations. It does not apply to systems that cannot be modeled with a Lagrangian alone, such as dissipative systems.

Classical Japanese dance-drama, performed since the 14th century. Noh is Japan's oldest major theater art still regularly performed. Its plays are often based on tales in which a supernatural being, transformed into a human hero, narrates the story. Chiefly unwanted, unintentional or harmful sound that is unpleasant, loud or disruptive to hearing or mental faculties. From a physics standpoint, noise and desired sound are both vibrations through a medium; the difference lies in how the brain perceives them. Sound is measured by amplitude, expressed in [[Decibel|decibels]] (dB), and by frequency, expressed in [[Hertz|hertz]] (Hz). Noise is often measured in dBA using A-weighting, where 0 dBA is the softest sound a person can hear, normal speech is about 65 dBA, and a rock concert can reach about 120 dBA. In audio engineering, noise also means residual hiss, rumble, crackle or hum, and in experimental science it denotes random fluctuations that obscure a signal. Environmental noise from road vehicles, aircraft, trains and industry causes annoyance and disrupted sleep, and it is linked to [[Hearing loss|hearing loss]] and cardiovascular disease.

Ancient civilization of central [[Nigeria|Nigeria]], named after the village of Nok in southern Kaduna State, where its terracotta sculptures were discovered in 1928. Nok culture may have emerged around 1500 BCE and persisted until about 1 BCE. Its people may have produced terracotta sculptures on a large scale as part of a complex funerary culture. The earliest sculptures may date to 900 BCE, and some figures wield slingshots, bows and arrows, which may indicate hunting. A sculpture of two paddlers in a dugout canoe suggests river transport along tributaries of the [[Niger River|Niger River]] and regional [[Trade|trade]]. Iron metallurgy may have developed independently between 750 BCE and 550 BCE, and settlements are often found on mountaintops.

Nomadic hunting and gathering is by far the oldest known human subsistence method. Pair of geometries based on axioms closely related to those specifying [[Euclidean geometry|Euclidean geometry]]. Non-Euclidean geometry arises by replacing [[Euclid|Euclid]]'s parallel postulate with an alternative. In hyperbolic geometry, infinitely many lines through a point not on a given line fail to intersect it, and the lines diverge; in elliptic geometry, every such line intersects the given line, and lines converge. Mathematicians tried for at least a thousand years to prove the parallel postulate from the other four, including [[Ibn al-Haytham|Ibn al-Haytham]] and Omar Khayyám. Lobachevsky published the first rigorous non-Euclidean system in 1829–1830, and Bolyai formulated a similar one in 1832. Non-Euclidean geometry underpins Albert Einstein's general theory of relativity (1915), in which spacetime is modeled as a curved [[Riemannian manifold|Riemannian manifold]].
