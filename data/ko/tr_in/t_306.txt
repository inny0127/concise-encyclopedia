Chinese royal dynasty of the 2nd millennium BC that ruled the Yellow River valley, traditionally succeeding the [[Xia dynasty]] and followed by the [[Zhou dynasty|Western Zhou dynasty]]. Modern scholarship dates it between the 16th and 11th centuries BC. It is the earliest dynasty in traditional Chinese history firmly supported by archaeological evidence. Excavations at Yinxu, near modern Anyang and the final Shang capital of Yin, have revealed royal tombs, palace foundations, and the remains of humans and animals sacrificed in state rituals. More than 20,000 oracle bones were discovered in the 1920s and 1930s, and the inscriptions shed light on politics, economy and religious practice.

Sharks range from the dwarf lanternshark, 17 centimeters long, to the [[Whale shark]], the largest fish in the world, reaching 12 to 18.8 meters. He renamed Humayun's Dina-panah city as Shergarh and revived ancient Pataliputra as [[Patna]]. His campaigns brought Punjab, Malwa, Marwar, Mewar and Bundelkhand under his control. His reorganization of the empire laid foundations for later Mughal rulers, notably [[Akbar]]. He died while besieging Kalinjar fort, and the empire descended into civil war before the Mughals reconquered it.

Second-largest branch of [[Islam]], defined by the belief that [[Muhammad]] designated his cousin and son-in-law [[Ali|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] (r. 656–661) as his successor.

Shia Muslims hold that Ali was the rightful political successor (caliph) and the divinely guided spiritual leader (imam) of the Muslim community. They maintain that his right was unjustly usurped at the meeting of Saqifa, where [[Abu Bakr]] (r. 632–634) was chosen as caliph. Sunni Muslims accept Abu Bakr, [[Umar]] (r. 634–644), and [[Uthman]] (r. 644–656). Shia Muslims are estimated at 10–13% of the world's Muslims, roughly 200–260 million. They form a majority in [[Iran]], Iraq, and Azerbaijan and almost half the Muslim population of Lebanon and Bahrain. Iran is the only country where Shia Islam officially underlies both the legal framework and the system of governance.
