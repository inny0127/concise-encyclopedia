ENTRY:
Daylight illumination from diffuse sky radiation while the [[Sun]] is below the [[Horizon|horizon]], as sunlight from the upper atmosphere is scattered. The sky dims as the Sun sinks, and illumination is nearly zero when it reaches 18° below the horizon. Civil twilight, when the Sun's center is between the horizon and 6° below it, gives enough light that artificial light is unnecessary for most outdoor activity. Photographers and painters call it the blue hour, from the French *l'heure bleue*. Metaphorically, twilight implies something losing strength and nearing its end.

During the [[American Civil War]], she served as a training ship for the Naval Academy. Designated a museum ship in 1907, she has sailed under her own power on special anniversaries.

Variable-width character encoding for [[Unicode]] that is backward compatible with [[ASCII]]. The name is short for Unicode Transformation Format – 8-bit. Former U.S. Highway running from [[Chicago]] to Santa Monica, California. German submarine, especially one operated by the navy in [[World War I]] or [[World War II]]. The term anglicizes *Unterseeboot* ("undersea boat"). U-boats waged unrestricted submarine warfare against the [[United Kingdom]] to disrupt merchant traffic. In World War II, [[Karl Dönitz]] focused on convoy battles in the [[Battle of the Atlantic]], but improved tactics turned the tide, and by May 1943 losses outpaced U-boat sinkings.
