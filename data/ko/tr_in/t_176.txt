Reaction engine that produces thrust by expelling a fast-moving jet of heated gas, usually air. Air-breathing jet engines, including turbojets, [[Turbofan|turbofans]], ramjets and scramjets, are internal combustion engines. Typically they have a rotating compressor powered by a turbine, with leftover power providing thrust through a nozzle, a process known as the Brayton thermodynamic cycle. Early jets used relatively inefficient turbojets for subsonic flight, so most modern subsonic jet aircraft use high-bypass turbofans, which give higher speed and greater fuel efficiency than propeller engines over long distances. Decorative items worn for personal adornment, such as brooches, rings, necklaces, earrings, pendants, bracelets, and cufflinks. The American spelling is *jewelry*. The word derives from *jewel*, which entered English from Old French *jouel* and ultimately from Latin *jocale*, meaning "plaything."

From a Western perspective the term is restricted to durable ornaments. For many centuries gold and silver, often combined with [[Gemstone|gemstones]], have been the standard materials, while [[Amber|amber]], [[Coral|coral]], glass, shells, wood, bone, ivory, clay, and enamel are also used. Platinum alloys range from 900 (90% pure) to 950 (95% pure), and the silver used is usually sterling silver, 92.5% fine. Makers employ forging, casting, soldering or welding, cutting, carving, and "cold-joining" with adhesives, staples, and rivets. Beads, including the small seed beads used in woven styles, are common, and beadwork is prominent in many African and indigenous North American cultures.

Jewelry is among the oldest types of archaeological artefact, and perforated shell beads are cited as early evidence of personal decoration. Its uses include fixing clothing or hair in place, marking social status (as with wedding rings and chains of office), signifying ethnic, religious, or social affiliation (as with the Christian crucifix or the Jewish Star of David), providing talismanic protection through amulets, artistic display, and carrying personal meaning such as love, mourning, or luck. Many items, such as brooches and buckles, began as purely functional objects and became decorative. Some cultures store wedding dowries as jewelry, and it has served as currency or a trade good.

[[Diamond|Diamonds]] were first mined in [[India]]. In 2005 Australia, Botswana, Russia, and Canada ranked among the primary sources of gemstone diamond production.

Jurchen-led imperial dynasty of China, ruled by the Wanyan clan (1115–1234). The dynasty emerged from Wanyan Aguda's rebellion against the [[Liao dynasty]], and its official name, Great Jin, means "gold." After conquering Liao territory, the Jin waged a century-long campaign against the [[Song dynasty]] in southern China. Its emperors adopted Han customs, fortified the Great Wall against the rising [[Mongol Empire]], and oversaw a revival of [[Confucianism]]. In 1211 the Mongols, led by [[Genghis Khan]], invaded and won several victories. Over 23 years the Jin faced defeats, revolts, defections and coups, and the Mongols finally conquered them in 1234.
