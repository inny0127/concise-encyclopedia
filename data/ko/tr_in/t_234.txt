Ancient kingdom in southern China and [[Vietnam|northern Vietnam]], annexed by the [[Han dynasty]] in 111 BC. It was founded by the Chinese general Zhao Tuo, who had commanded Nanhai Commandery under the [[Qin dynasty]] and established the kingdom after the dynasty's collapse. Its territory covered the modern Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, [[Hong Kong]], [[Macau]] and southern Fujian regions. Its rulers were of Han Chinese origin and introduced bureaucracy and writing to the region. Vietnamese historians have disagreed over whether Nanyue was a legitimate Vietnamese state, and its rulers are known there as the Triệu dynasty.

Incendiary mixture of a gelling agent and a volatile petrochemical, usually gasoline or diesel fuel. The name is a portmanteau of the aluminum salts of naphthenic and palmitic acids that were its original thickeners. A team led by chemist Louis Fieser developed it in 1942 for the U.S. Chemical Warfare Service in a secret laboratory at [[Harvard University]]. Napalm burns at 800 to 1,200 °C (1,500 to 2,200 °F), longer than gasoline, and adheres to its targets. Its largest use was in airdropped incendiary bombs against Japanese cities in 1945, during [[World War II]]. French military commander and statesman, Emperor of the French (r. 1804–14, 1815). Born Napoleone di Buonaparte on the island of [[Corsica]] to a family of Italian origin, he was commissioned in the French Royal Army in 1785. He supported the [[French Revolution|Revolution]] and rose rapidly after the siege of Toulon in 1793, then won decisive victories against the Austrians and their Italian allies in 1796. After an invasion of Egypt and Syria in 1798, he engineered the Coup of 18 Brumaire in November 1799 and became First Consul. He sold Louisiana to the United States in 1803 and crowned himself emperor in December 1804.

His victory at the [[Battle of Austerlitz]] in 1805 led to the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire. His invasion of the Iberian Peninsula in 1808, which installed his brother Joseph as King of Spain, provoked the [[Peninsular War]]. After the invasion of Russia in 1812 and a catastrophic winter retreat, he was decisively defeated at the [[Battle of Leipzig]] in 1813 and abdicated in April 1814, going into exile on Elba. He escaped about ten months later and reclaimed France in the [[Hundred Days]], but the Seventh Coalition defeated him at the [[Battle of Waterloo]] in June 1815. He was exiled to Saint Helena, where he died.

His reforms include the [[Napoleonic Code]], a system of public education, the abolition of the vestiges of feudalism, and the emancipation of Jews and other religious minorities. Critics point to his abolition of the free press, the end of directly elected representative government, the reinstatement of slavery in French colonies, and the return of a hereditary monarchy and nobility. One estimate puts deaths from the Napoleonic Wars at 3 to 6 million.

French civil code established in 1804 during the French Consulate, and still in force in [[France]]. Officially the Civil Code of the French, it was drafted by a commission of four jurists, though [[Napoleon]] chaired many of its plenary sessions, and his support was crucial to its enactment. Its stress on clear, accessible law was a major milestone in abolishing the patchwork of feudal laws. Historian Robert Holtman regards it as one of the few documents to have influenced the whole world. It was the first modern legal code adopted with a pan-European scope, and it strongly influenced countries formed during and after the [[Napoleonic Wars]]. It also shaped legal reform in the [[Middle East]], while in [[Latin America]] Spain and Portugal had already established their own civil codes. Its categories derived from Justinian's [[Corpus Juris Civilis]].

Solution concept in non-cooperative [[Game theory|game theory]]. A set of strategies is a Nash equilibrium when no player can gain by changing their own strategy while the others keep theirs unchanged. The idea dates to 1838, when Cournot applied it to his model of competition in an oligopoly. John Nash showed that a Nash equilibrium exists, possibly in mixed strategies, for every finite game. The concept is widely used in [[Economics|economics]], and equilibria can be inefficient, as in the [[Prisoner's dilemma|prisoner's dilemma]], where mutual defection results despite a better cooperative outcome.
