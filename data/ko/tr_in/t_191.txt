Austrian-born logician, mathematician and philosopher. His [[Gödel's incompleteness theorems|incompleteness theorems]], published in 1931, showed that a consistent formal axiomatic system capable of describing [[Natural number|natural numbers]] cannot prove its own consistency and cannot decide all statements about them. Gödel proved his completeness theorem in 1929 in his doctoral dissertation at the [[University of Vienna|University of Vienna]].
