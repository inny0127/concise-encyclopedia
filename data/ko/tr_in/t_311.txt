Cancer that arises from the skin, most often caused by [[Ultraviolet|ultraviolet]] exposure. Skin cancer is the most common form of cancer, globally accounting for at least 40% of cases. The three main types are basal-cell and squamous-cell carcinomas, together called nonmelanoma skin cancer, and [[Melanoma|melanoma]], the most aggressive. Decreasing ultraviolet exposure and using sunscreen appear effective at preventing melanoma and squamous-cell skin cancer.

ENTRY:
Ownership of a person as property, especially in regard to their labor. Slavery typically involves compulsory work, with the location of work and residence dictated by the party holding the person in bondage. Enslavement occurred through law-breaking, debt, military defeat, or exploitation for cheaper labor, and some forms were instituted along lines of race or sex. Slaves were held for life or for a fixed period. In chattel slavery, the slave is legally the personal property of the owner and can be bought and sold.

Slavery existed in most societies and was a common feature of civilization. It was a keystone of society in ancient Greece and the [[Roman Empire]], and was practiced in the medieval Muslim world, sub-Saharan Africa, the Caribbean, and the antebellum United States. The transatlantic trade carried about 12 million Africans to the Americas between 1500 and 1866.

From the 18th century, [[Abolitionism|abolitionist]] movements in Europe and the Americas condemned slavery as a violation of human rights. After the [[French Revolution]] France abolished slavery in 1794, Napoleon reintroduced it in 1802, and permanent abolition came in 1848. Slavery was abolished in much of the [[British Empire]] in 1833, throughout the United States in 1865, in [[Cuba]] in 1886, and in [[Brazil]], the last country in the Americas to do so, in 1888.

Slavery is now outlawed in every country except as punishment for a crime, but persists in some form everywhere. In 2019 approximately 40 million people, 26% of them children, were enslaved, most often in forced labor, debt bondage, forced marriage, or [[Human trafficking|human trafficking]].

Systems of servitude that were widespread in historical Africa, both indigenous and for export. Slavery was part of African economic structures for centuries. Indigenous forms included debt slavery, enslavement of war captives, military slavery and enslavement of criminals. Earlier Arab-oriented trades across the Sahara and East Africa were estimated at 10 to 18 million over 1,200 years, though figures are debated.

Landlocked country in Central Europe, officially the Slovak Republic, with a population exceeding 5.4 million. Its capital and largest city is [[Bratislava]]. Its territory was part of the Kingdom of Hungary before it became part of Czechoslovakia, which was proclaimed in 1918. After a Soviet-backed coup in 1948, Czechoslovakia became a communist state, and the Velvet Revolution ended communist rule in 1989. Slovakia became independent on 1 January 1993 after the peaceful dissolution of Czechoslovakia. It is a member of the [[European Union]], [[NATO]], the [[Schengen Area]] and the [[OECD]], and it is home to eight UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Known as the "European Detroit," it is the world's largest per-capita car producer, making 1.08 million cars in 2023, with the automotive sector accounting for 44% of industrial output.

Country in Central Europe, bordered by Italy, Austria, Hungary and Croatia. Its capital and largest city is [[Ljubljana]]. Mostly mountainous and forested, it covers 20,271 sq km (7,827 sq mi) and has a population of about 2.1 million. Its official language is Slovene. [[Slavs|Slavic tribes]], the ancestors of modern Slovenes, arrived in the 5th century AD, and the territory later belonged to many states, including the Kingdom of Hungary and the Habsburg Empire. Slovenia became one of six constituent republics of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia in 1945 and declared independence in June 1991, after the brief Ten-Day War. Its high-income economy spans manufacturing, information technology, financial services and tourism, and the country is a member of the [[European Union]] and [[NATO]].

Nocturnal primate of the genus *Nycticebus*, native to [[Southeast Asia]]. Slow lorises range from [[Bangladesh]] and northeast India to the [[Philippines]], and from China's Yunnan province to [[Java]]. At least eight species are recognized, and a ninth, the pygmy slow loris, was moved to the genus *Xanthonycticebus*. They have large eyes, a round head and a toxic bite, produced by licking a sweat gland on the arm and mixing the secretion with saliva; the toxin is used mainly in disputes within the species. They move slowly and deliberately, and when threatened they stop and remain motionless. All slow lorises are threatened by the wildlife trade and habitat loss, and demand from the exotic pet trade and [[Traditional medicine|traditional medicine]] has been the greatest cause of decline.

ENTRY:
Densely populated urban residential area of weak housing, often associated with [[Poverty|poverty]]. Slum residences range from shanties to deteriorated but professionally built dwellings, and most lack reliable sanitation, clean water, electricity and law enforcement. The [[United Nations]] defines slums as informal settlements lacking one or more of the following: access to improved water, access to improved sanitation, sufficient living area, housing durability and security of tenure. Slums became common in the United States and Europe in the 19th to late 20th centuries and are still predominantly found in developing countries, where the world's largest slum city is Orangi in [[Karachi]], Pakistan. Causes include rapid rural-to-urban migration, high unemployment, poor planning and natural disasters. Strategies include slum removal, relocation, upgrading and public housing.
