ENTRY:
Statistical hypothesis test for comparing observed and expected frequencies in categorical data. Published by [[Karl Pearson|Karl Pearson]] in 1900, Pearson's chi-squared test is considered one of the foundations of modern statistics. It determines whether there is a statistically significant difference between expected and observed frequencies in one or more categories of a contingency table, and it can test whether two categorical variables are independent. Domesticated subspecies (*Gallus gallus domesticus*) of the [[Red junglefowl|red junglefowl]], originally native to [[Southeast Asia]]. One of the most common and widespread domesticated animals in the world, it is cultivated primarily as [[Poultry|poultry]] for meat and [[Egg|eggs]] and occasionally kept as a pet. As of 2023, the global live population exceeded 26.5 billion, with more than 50 billion birds slaughtered annually for consumption. Specialized [[Breed|breeds]] have been developed for meat and egg yield, and a hen bred for egg production can lay over 300 eggs per year. Critics of [[Intensive farming|industrial-scale rearing]] raise welfare concerns over confined conditions, though assessments vary with management practices.

The word comes from Old English *ċicen*, and historically meant a young bird, as in Shakespeare's [[Macbeth]], where Macduff laments the death of "all my pretty chickens and their dam." A hen is an adult female; a rooster or cock, a fertile adult male.

Chickens are relatively large, day-active birds with round bodies, short wings, and, in most breeds, unfeathered legs. Domestic chickens are too heavy to fly more than a short distance. Chicks weigh about 37 g at hatching; by day 35 a Ross 708 broiler may weigh 1.8 kg, against 1.05 kg for a heritage chicken. Omnivores, they may live 5–10 years; the oldest known lived 16 years.

Chickens are gregarious and live in flocks with a dominance hierarchy, called the "pecking order" after its description in female chickens by Thorleif Schjelderup-Ebbe in 1921. They use varied vocalizations, including different warning calls for aerial and ground predators. Their cognition, emotions, and sociality are comparable with those of other birds and mammals.

Global production reached approximately 37 million metric tons in 2022, led by China with about 45% of output.

Cartilaginous fish in the order Chimaeriformes. Chimaeras, also called ghost sharks or ratfish, are soft-bodied, shark-like fish with bulky heads and long, tapered tails, reaching up to 150 cm (4.9 ft). Like other members of the class [[Chondrichthyes]], their skeletons are entirely [[Cartilage|cartilaginous]]. Their closest living relatives are [[Shark|sharks]] and rays, from which their last common ancestor diverged nearly 400 million years ago. The group was once diverse and abundant, and fossils from the [[Carboniferous]] period, over 300 million years ago, document chimaeroid forms. Living species, apart from plow-nose chimaeras, are largely confined to deep water, some below 2,000 m (6,600 ft). Instead of sharks' replaceable teeth, they have six permanent tooth-plates that form a beaklike crushing mechanism, comparable to the incisors of [[Rodent|rodents]]. Some chimaeras have a [[Venom|venomous]] spine on the front of the dorsal fin.

Architectural ventilation structure of masonry, clay, or metal, for hot exhaust gases and smoke. Chimneys are built as near vertical as possible so that rising gases draw air into [[Combustion|combustion]] through the stack effect. The space inside is called the flue. Chimneys carry gases from a [[Stove|stove]], [[Fireplace|fireplace]], boiler, or furnace away from living areas. Domestic chimneys first appeared in large dwellings in northern Europe in the 12th century, and the earliest surviving English example, at the keep of [[Castle|Conisbrough Castle]] in [[Yorkshire]], dates from 1185. Chimneys became common in ordinary houses only in the 16th and 17th centuries. In the 18th and 19th centuries, the extraction of [[Lead|lead]] from ore produced toxic fumes, and long near-horizontal chimneys in northern England, often more than 3 km (2 mi) long, carried them to a remote vertical stack.

Political grouping of the Chimú culture on the northern coast of Peru. The Chimú culture arose about 900 CE, succeeding the [[Moche culture]], and developed on a desert strip where rivers carved fertile valleys suited to irrigation. Chimor was the largest kingdom of the Late Intermediate Period, encompassing 1,000 km (620 mi) of coastline. Its capital, Chan Chan, was founded, according to legend, by Taycanamo. Irrigation [[Canal|canals]] extended up to 80 km, and the Chimú are known for distinctive monochromatic [[Pottery|pottery]], usually with a shiny black finish, and for metalwork in [[Gold|gold]] and [[Silver|silver]]. The Chimú worshipped the [[Moon]] and traded Spondylus shells from the coast of present-day Ecuador. The [[Inca Empire]] conquered Chimor around 1470, 50 years before the Spanish arrived in the region.

Medium-sized great ape species of equatorial Africa. Chimpanzees live in forests and savannas, and four subspecies are confirmed. They share the genus *Pan* with the [[Bonobo]], and fossil and DNA evidence show that the two species are the closest living relatives of [[Human|humans]]. Chimpanzees eat mainly fruit, but also insects, and will hunt smaller monkeys. Nearly all populations use tools, such as modified sticks, for hunting and for obtaining [[Honey]], [[Termite|termites]], and [[Ant|ants]]. Groups can number more than 200 members, with a male-dominated hierarchy in which disputes are generally settled without violence. The species is listed as [[Endangered species|endangered]] on the IUCN Red List, with habitat loss, poaching, and disease as the main threats. Some hundreds have been kept in laboratories, mainly in the United States. Attempts to teach them sign language have been made, but academics such as [[Noam Chomsky]] have questioned whether they can acquire language.

National space agency of [[China|the People's Republic of China]]. Founded in 1993 and headquartered in Haidian, Beijing, the agency manages China's civil space programs and international space cooperation. It is a national bureau under the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. Its Chang'e program made China the first space agency to land on the [[Far side of the Moon|far side of the Moon]], with Chang'e 4, and returned lunar material with Chang'e 5 and 6. Since the Wolf Amendment of 2011, Congress has required [[NASA]] to exclude cooperation with the agency, although this has been periodically overcome.
